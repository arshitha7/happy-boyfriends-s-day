# Happy Boyfriend's Day ♡ — October 3, 2026

An intimate, interactive animated romantic short film created for Boyfriend's Day on October 3, 2026.

## ✨ The Story
- **Scene 1 (Opening):** Peaceful park at golden hour under warm sunset skies with gentle sunbeams and floating petals.
- **Scene 2 (Waiting):** He sits relaxed on a wooden bench under the trees.
- **Scene 3 (The Surprise):** She walks down the sunlit path secretly hiding a bouquet of fresh red roses behind her back.
- **Scene 4 & 5 (The Roses):** She sits beside him, surprises him with the roses, and he accepts with a warm smile.
- **Scene 6 (The Conversation):** Intimate dialogue appears one by one in a cinematic frosted glass dialogue box while keeping both characters completely visible.
- **Hand-Holding Moment:** As the dialogue reaches holding hands forever, their hands intertwine on the bench with a soft glowing heart.
- **The Question & Escaping NO Button:** A playful interactive question card appears. The "NO" button playfully escapes any attempt to click or hover, leaving only "YES".
- **The Celebration & Ending:** A warm romantic celebration of flower petals, sunset pull-back under the great tree, and eternal vows.

## 🚀 How to Run
No Node.js, npm, servers, or build tools required.
Simply double-click `index.html` or open it in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).
